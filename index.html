The problem

The work is done. The money is somewhere in your inbox.

When you run the business side alone, the simple questions take the longest to answer.

"Who still owes me?"

Amounts live across emails, a spreadsheet and your memory, so you never quite trust the total.

"Did I already remind them?"

Follow-ups slip, or you send them late because writing the message feels awkward.

"What happened to that quote?"

Proposals go out and nobody tracks which ones turned into paid work.

How it works

One flow, from first quote to paid.

Every record is linked, so totals, due dates and reminder stages update on their own.

  1. Quote

    Track proposals, their value and when to follow up. See your win rate.

  2. Project

    Link won work to a project with tasks, dates and its open invoice total.

  3. Invoice

    Log what you billed. The due date comes from the client's payment terms.

  4. Paid

    Record full or partial payments. Overdue lists and income by month update.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. It's built to work on Notion's free plan. You duplicate the template into your own workspace.

What do I get after I pay?

Gumroad emails you a receipt with a link to the template. Open it, click Duplicate (top right) and follow the 5-minute "Start here" page.

Does it create tax invoices or receipts?

No. SoloClerk tracks what you billed and what's been paid. Keep issuing official documents with your usual invoicing or accounting tool.

Can I track shekels as well as dollars?

Yes. Each invoice can be in USD or ILS.

What if a client pays only part of an invoice?

Record the amount paid. The open balance shows what's still owed, and the invoice stays on your list until it's paid in full.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes, through the Notion app. Setup is easiest on a computer, and day-to-day checks work fine on your phone.

Are the numbers in the screenshots real?

No. They're sample data included so you can see how the views work. Every database has a "Sample data" view, so clearing them takes a minute.

Is my data private?

Your copy lives in your own Notion workspace. SoloClerk has no access to it.
